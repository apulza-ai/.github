## Hi there, welcome to Apulza 👋

Apulza is an AI-driven student support platform focused on 
making medical education and research more accessible and intelligent.

### 🧠 What we're building
- An AI-powered assistant trained on medical research data
- Tools to help medical students learn faster and smarter
- A platform bridging the gap between research and education

### 🛠️ Our Tech Stack (Tentative)
- Frontend: React / Next.js
- Backend: Laravel / Node.js
- AI: OpenBioLLM fine-tuned as ApulzaLLM
- Database: Supabase / PostgreSQL

### 👥 Teams
- SWE - Building the platform
- Machine Learning - Building the AI models
- UI/UX Design - Crafting the experience  
- Research - Powering the intelligence
- Marketing - Promoting the company
